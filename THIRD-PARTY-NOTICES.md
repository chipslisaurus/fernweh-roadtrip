## Europe data (1.5)

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
License: Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Road routes and distances retrieved through OSRM on 2026-09-26. Road bearings and selected district footprints are adapted, compressed, and simplified for the game. Reverse-direction distances are approximated. Fictional transfers are explicitly marked; their distances are approximate straight-line measurements.

The derived machine-readable database is provided under ODbL 1.0 in data/Fernweh-Europe-ODbL.json, both in the game ZIP and public repository. This licensing notice applies to that database, not the entire game's source code.

Country boundaries: Natural Earth, public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
OSRM API: https://project-osrm.org/docs/v5.24.0/
